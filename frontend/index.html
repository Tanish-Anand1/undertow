FAQ

The short answers

What sources are live?

Hacker News, GitHub, and X. Sudo listens there now. Reddit is next, not live yet.

Is Reddit coming?

Yes. It is wired in the product, held until credentials and rate limits are honest. You will see it as a source when it is actually fetching.

Do drafts send themselves?

No. Sudo drafts. You edit. You send. Nothing leaves without you.

How does shared-crawl matching work?

Each unique keyword is crawled once. If three founders watch “auth friction,” Sudo does not fetch it three times. The post is stored once, then linked to every watchlist that shares the term.

What do you keep?

Public posts matched to your keywords, your account, and the drafts you ask for. We do not scrape LinkedIn. We do not crawl the whole web. We do not sell the feed.
